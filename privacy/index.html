Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 11, 2026

Relivance LLC ("Relivance," "we," "us") operates relivance.app. This page explains what we collect on this website and what we do with it. The Relivance iOS app is not yet released; this site is a pre-launch information page.

What we collect on this site

The only personal information we collect on relivance.app is the email address you choose to submit through the early-access form, along with the date and time of submission.

We do not run analytics, advertising trackers, or fingerprinting scripts on this site. We do not use cookies for tracking. Standard server logs at our hosting provider may briefly record routine request data (IP address, browser type, referring page) for security and abuse-prevention purposes.

How we use your email

We use the email address you submit only to:

  • Send you early-access updates about Relivance.
  • Invite you to use the app when private early access opens.
  • Send launch-related announcements.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your email address. We do not use it for unrelated marketing.

Third-party services we use

Form submissions on this site are handled by a third-party form provider (currently Formspree, Inc., based in the United States). Formspree receives the email address you submit, processes it on our behalf, and forwards it to our internal early-access list. Formspree's own privacy practices are described at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., a Microsoft subsidiary, based in the United States). GitHub may keep standard request logs for the static files we publish.

The Roboto typeface used on this site is self-hosted from relivance.app itself. We do not load fonts, stylesheets, or scripts from third-party CDNs such as Google Fonts, so your IP address is not shared with a font provider when you visit a page.

Because Formspree and GitHub operate from the United States, submitting the early-access form may involve transferring limited technical data (your email address, your IP address) outside your home jurisdiction. We rely on these providers' standard contractual safeguards for those transfers. If you would prefer not to share this data, the simplest option is to not submit the early-access form.

How long we keep your email

We keep early-access email addresses until you ask us to remove them, or until twelve months after Relivance has publicly launched and the early-access list is no longer in active use, whichever comes first.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your email address from the early-access list at any time by writing to privacy@relivance.app. Every email-update we send will also include an unsubscribe link.

If you are in the EU, UK, or another region with comparable data protection laws, you have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Reach out at privacy@relivance.app and we'll act on your request promptly.

Children

Relivance is intended for adults. This site is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect email addresses from anyone under 16. If you believe we have, please contact us and we will delete the record.

Changes

We may update this policy as Relivance evolves. The "last updated" date at the top will change when we do. Material changes will be announced to the early-access list.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your personal information? Email us at privacy@relivance.app.
